# My project brief
## The question
How accessible is each settlement area in Owerri West to health facilities?

## Why it matters
Access to health facilties is important because it directly affects how quick residents receieve medical care, especially during emergencies. 
Assessing accessibility across settlement areas in Owerri west can help identify communities in need of health facilities and support better planning and distribution of healthcare resources.

### The data I need 
- LGA boundaries - OSM - https://www.openstreetmap.org/export#map=10/5.5367/7.4556 
- Settlment points - GRID3 - https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-settlement-extents-v4-1/about
- Road Networks - OSM - https://www.openstreetmap.org/export#map=10/5.5367/7.4556 
- Health facilities - https://www.openstreetmap.org/export#map=10/5.5367/7.4556 
- WorldPop Hub - https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/

#### What would you build
I would build a GIS based haelthcare accessibilty system for Owerri West LGA that shows the locations of settlements, health facilities and the road network within the study area. 
The system would analyse the road network to determine the nearest healthcare facility and calculate the distance and estimated travel time from each settlement to the facility.
The results would be used to classify settlments based on their level of healthcare accessibility and identify communities that lack health facilities and may require improved healthcare services or additional facilities.
