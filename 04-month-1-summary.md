## Question

Which settlement areas in Owerri West LGA are within 1 km of a health facility?

## Operation

I performed a 1 km buffer analysis around the GRID3 health facilities in Owerri West LGA.

The buffer was then used to identify settlement areas located within 1 km of at least one health facility. 
To visually identify the settlement areas covered by the buffer, I overlaid the buffer layer on Google imagery.

## Expected

I expected approximately 60% of the settlement areas in Owerri West LGA to fall within the 1 km coverage area of the health facilities.
